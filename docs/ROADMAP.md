# Roadmap

Planned work is tracked as [GitHub issues](https://github.com/TheRealestNwah/fitness-app/issues); each issue describes when it's done. This page groups the open ones by priority. Open [bugs](https://github.com/TheRealestNwah/fitness-app/issues?q=is%3Aopen+label%3Abug) always come first.

## Shipped

Stride already covers the core of a weight-loss journey:

- **Plan:** onboarding with BMR/TDEE, a safe calorie target and macros; adaptive maintenance estimate; flexible weekly budget and diet breaks; maintenance mode.
- **Logging:** food diary with a built-in database, fuzzy search, barcode and nutrition-label scanning, Open Food Facts, favourites, copy yesterday, photo meals, recipes with scaling and URL import; weigh-ins with progress photos; vitals and body measurements; water; exercise with earn-back calories; fasting timer with a Live Activity.
- **Insight:** weight trend and rate of loss, milestones and plateau detection, weekly review, correlations, shareable summaries and a doctor-friendly PDF.
- **Everywhere:** Apple Health sync, iCloud sync, widgets and controls, Apple Watch app, Siri and Shortcuts, Home Screen quick actions, app lock, iPad sidebar layout.
